How it works

Four steps, from your values to a result you can act on.

One interface, nine models

Each condition has its own estimator, its own input schema and its own decision threshold. The interface is shared so the experience is identical whichever you screen for.

Screenings

Nine conditions, each with its own trained model.

Accuracy is measured on a held-out 20% test split, stratified by class, never used during training or tuning.

Training approach

Each module was tuned with 5-fold Grid Search cross-validation. SMOTE was applied to the training split only — never before the split — so no synthetic sample can leak into evaluation. Models generalise closely, with under 8% between training and test accuracy across all seven algorithms compared.

Validation

The full numbers, including the weak ones.

Accuracy ranges from 78.5% to 96.5% across modules. The spread is real and reflects genuine differences in dataset size and task difficulty — the diabetes module is the hardest of the nine.

Limitations

What this system does not do.

Stated plainly, because a screening tool that hides its boundaries is worse than one that names them.

Small training sets

All nine numerical modules train on 197–768 samples. These are the standard research benchmarks, but they do not represent the full diversity of clinical presentation.

Narrow demographics

The PIMA diabetes dataset is drawn entirely from Pima Indian women, which limits how far that module's performance generalises to other groups.

Rule-based explanations

Explanations come from clinical thresholds rather than model-native attribution such as SHAP or LIME. They are more readable, but they do not always mirror the model's internal logic.

No clinical validation

Nothing here has been prospectively validated in a clinical setting, and the system depends on values being entered honestly and correctly.

About

Open source, and accountable to its own numbers.

VITALS integrates nine disease modules and one 42-class symptom model behind a single interface — the broadest such integration among the open academic prototypes reviewed for this work. Every claim on this page is traceable to the published evaluation.
